About Me

I'm a cloud and DevOps engineer focused on building reliable, secure and maintainable cloud environments. I work with AWS services and infrastructure as code to make deployments more consistent and repeatable. My interests include cloud architecture, automation, CI/CD pipelines, containerisation and monitoring. I enjoy breaking down technical problems, learning how systems work and turning that knowledge into practical projects.

Through hands-on work with tools such as Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes and Linux, I'm developing my ability to deploy applications, manage infrastructure and improve operational reliability. I believe good engineering combines clear documentation, security-conscious decisions and continuous improvement. This portfolio showcases my projects, technical skills and progress as I continue to grow in cloud engineering and DevOps.
